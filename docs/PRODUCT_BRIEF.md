# MyBoon — product brief

**MyBoon sticks market context to the next action — understand why it moved, then act in the same app.**

## The problem

Crypto users already have plenty of information. What they lack is useful context at the right time, and a clean path from “I get why this moved” to “I can do something about it.”

Today that path is split across X, Telegram, Discord, dashboards, news, and trading apps. One surface shows a price spike. Another hints at a narrative. A dashboard shows volume. By the time someone understands the move, it’s often crowded or stale — and they still have to figure out which venue, asset, and app to use next.

## What MyBoon does

MyBoon is a mobile product built around Solana. It turns scattered market signals into researched, developing stories, connects each story to the relevant market, and keeps wallet balances and positions visible in the same app.

```
Market moves → MyBoon explains why → you open the relevant market → MyBoon tracks what happens next
```

Three layers, one loop:

| Layer | Job |
|-------|-----|
| **Context** | Short, useful narratives with receipts — what happened, why it matters now, what changed, what to watch |
| **Act** | Prediction markets, perps, swaps, and related surfaces without leaving the app |
| **Track** | Balances, open positions, venues, and outcomes together |

The bet is not “another trading terminal on a phone.” The bet is knowing what matters and why, then making the next step obvious.

## Who it’s for

First users are mobile-first people who already follow crypto narratives but don’t want five apps to understand and act on one move. Over time, the same product should make crypto feel more familiar to newer users by hiding jargon behind clear financial interactions.

## Why mobile / why Solana

Most of this behavior already happens on phones. Solana has speed, liquidity, wallets, and a real mobile distribution path (including Seeker). MyBoon is designed for that: quick to open, easy to scan, simple to act from — not a desktop terminal shrunk down.

## What exists today

MyBoon is a working beta built by a solo founder. It does not yet have external users or revenue. The near-term milestone is distributing the Android beta through Solana Mobile, onboarding the first users, and checking whether this connected loop actually cuts confusion and app-switching.

Under the hood, signals become research memory and entity-level stories so later context doesn’t start from zero. That machinery exists to ground the feed in receipts — not to be the story we lead with.

## What MyBoon is not

Not a generic crypto news feed. Not a raw dashboard. Not another trading terminal squeezed onto a phone.
