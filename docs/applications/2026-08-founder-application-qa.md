## Canonical One-Line Description

myboon tells you why the market moved and lets you act in the same app.

## Brief Project Description

myboon is a mobile product for understanding, acting on, and tracking crypto
markets through one consistent experience. It turns scattered signals into
researched, developing stories, connects each story to the relevant asset and
market, and keeps wallet balances and resulting positions visible in the same
app.

## What Are You Building, And Who Is It For?

I am building myboon for mobile-first crypto participants who already follow
market narratives but currently move between X, Telegram, news, dashboards, and
trading applications to understand one market move and decide what to do next.

The first user is already active in crypto. Over time, I want the same product to
make crypto easier for newer users by presenting protocols and financial actions
through clear, familiar interactions instead of expecting them to learn every
piece of crypto jargon.

## Why Did You Decide To Build This, And Why Now?

The problem is personal. I have lost money while learning crypto because the
information, terminology, and financial mechanics were difficult to understand.
Even with a technical background, I had to gather context from several sources
and then navigate separate applications to act.

Now is the right time because Solana has the speed, liquidity, protocols, wallet
infrastructure, and mobile distribution needed to support a connected consumer
experience. What is still missing is a product that helps users understand what
is happening before they act and then makes the next step clear.

## What Technologies Are You Using?

myboon uses Expo and React Native for the mobile application; TypeScript, Node.js,
and Hono for the API and services; Supabase and Postgres for durable data; and a
pnpm monorepo for shared packages. Wallet flows support external Solana wallets
and embedded wallets. The product integrates or is building around Solana market
surfaces including Phoenix, Pacifica, Meteora, and Jupiter-routed swaps, with a
Polymarket surface also present in the wider product.

The feed uses structured collectors, research agents, an entity manager, an
editor, and a publisher rather than sending raw headlines directly to the app.
Development uses GitHub, EAS, Codex, and Claude Code alongside conventional
testing and deployment tooling.

## How Do You Know People Need This Product?

I do not yet have enough user evidence to claim that the idea is validated. The
hypothesis comes from my own experience as a crypto user. I have personally lost
money while learning because the information, terminology, and financial
mechanics were difficult to understand. When a market moves, the explanation is
scattered across X, Telegram, news, and dashboards. Even after finding the
context, acting on it requires navigating separate protocols and unfamiliar
trading interfaces.

myboon addresses both parts of that journey. It organizes scattered signals
into researched, developing stories, then connects that context to the relevant
Solana market where users can act and track their positions through one
consistent mobile experience.

I regularly share product progress through the myboon X account. The next step
is onboarding beta users and testing whether this connected experience helps
them understand the market and navigate crypto products with less confusion and
fewer application switches.

## How Far Along Are You? Do You Have Users?

myboon is a working beta, not just a prototype. I have built the Expo mobile app,
wallet experience, signal and research pipeline, chronological Story feed,
market calendar, and action and position flows for perpetuals, swaps, and
liquidity products. The backend infrastructure is running, and I am preparing
the Android beta for distribution through Solana Mobile.

I do not yet have external users, meaningful usage metrics, or revenue. I am
active on the myboon X account to share progress and find early testers.
Onboarding the first beta users and validating their behavior are the next
milestones.

## Who Else Is Building In This Space, And What Are They Getting Wrong?

The closest alternatives are currently a stack: Kaito for crypto information,
Birdeye for market and on-chain data, and Jupiter or Axiom for execution and
portfolio tools.

I do not think they are building bad products; they solve different parts of the
workflow. Research products help users understand, while trading products help
them execute. Users still have to carry the context between them.

myboon focuses on connecting those layers: turning scattered signals into a
researched, developing story, connecting it to the relevant asset and market,
and tracking the resulting position in one mobile experience.

## How Do You Make Money, Or How Do You Plan To?

myboon is currently pre-revenue. The initial model is affiliate or revenue-share
fees from actions routed to integrated venues, including perpetuals, swaps, and
other market products. Longer term, the accumulated research and context layer
can be offered as a usage-based API through mechanisms such as x402. The
immediate priority is validating retention and action intent before optimizing
monetization.
