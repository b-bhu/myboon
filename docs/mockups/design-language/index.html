Colour roles

  • Feed: screen walletCore, story cards surface, glance cards ground.
  • Apps: screen surface, cards surface 2, icon tiles lift. Blue only on the Apps top band.
  • Wallet: gold floods the header; dark scroll panel below.
  • Gold is restrained: small action links, bottom-tab active icon and label, MAX, unread square, primary outline buttons on MyBoon screens. Never a fill outside Wallet's header.
  • Viridian and vermillion carry meaning (up/down, success/error), never decoration.
  • Blue or green as a general accent is not allowed. Colours come only from tokens.ts.

Typography

  • Text uses the system font (Roboto on Android). No custom font.
  • Numbers (prices, balances, percentages) are monospace.
  • Small labels and timestamps are monospace, uppercase, 1px letter spacing, faint.
  • Only these sizes: 34 big numbers, 28 screen title (once per screen), 20 section header, 18 card title, 15 reading, 14 secondary, 12 captions and links, 11 mono labels.

Shape and depth

  • Cards 8, buttons and app tiles 6, bottom sheet top corners 12, chips 4.
  • 1px hairline border on cards, tiles and sheets.
  • No shadows. Depth comes from the background ladder.
  • No coloured side stripes or left-border accents, anywhere.

Tabs

  • In-screen tabs are always a segmented control: one bordered box, 6 corners, 2 inner padding.
  • Active segment is filled with lift and text is bone; inactive text is dim.
  • No underline tabs and no chip tabs. Filter lists with a segmented control or search.
  • Bottom tab bar: outline icons; active icon and label gold.

Buttons

  • Primary is an outline: 1px border and text, no fill, 6 corners, 15 semibold, 48 tall.
  • Primary takes the context accent: gold on MyBoon's own screens, the app's brand colour inside an integrated app (Polymarket, Phoenix, Meteora…). Brand colours live in one map.
  • Secondary is a hairline border outline with bone text. Tertiary is a text link in the context accent.
  • One primary per screen.

Swipe-to-confirm

  • Used for every trade and send.
  • Outline track 52 tall, 6 corners, in the context accent; solid square 44 knob with an arrow.
  • Label states the action and amount.
  • Track fills faintly behind the knob; releasing before the end snaps back. Completion fires the confirm haptic.

  • Money amounts use the amount-first card: mono label, 34 mono bold number, token and gold MAX on the right, mono balance line under.

Alerts and toasts

  • Success: a toast drops from the top (lift fill, hairline, 6 corners) with a viridian outline icon and a gold “View” link. Auto-dismisses after 3s; swipe up to dismiss.
  • Errors: an inline banner on the screen where the problem is, with a vermillion outline icon and a gold fix link. It stays until fixed.
  • Never coloured side stripes or left-border accents.

Loading and empty

  • Lists load with skeleton cards: boxed cards with lift bars shaped like title, text and meta. No full-screen spinners.
  • A spinner appears only inside a button while its action runs.
  • Empty state: centred faint outline icon (32), 18 title, one 14 dim line, one outline primary button.
