Detail · the screen you demo

Same frame on every venue. Only the chart body swaps.

  • Header is two lines, not a block. Price, change, one stat. Today's screens spend a third of the viewport before the chart starts.
  • The chart gets the space back. Range tabs are a compact strip, not a full segmented row — the News toggle sits on the same line.
  • Readout above the plot, in a fixed row. A floating tooltip sits under your thumb.
  • RSI is collapsed with its current value visible on the closed header, so you get the number without the pane. One tap opens it; the choice is remembered.
  • News rail under the chart. Markers on the plot, one card below. Hidden entirely when a market has no stories — no reserved empty space.
  • Action bar is pinned, never scrolls away. Two buttons, venue-worded: Long/Short on perps, Back YES/NO on Polymarket.
Why the sheet, not a screen. Sizing a trade is a decision about the thing you are already looking at. Pushing a new screen loses the chart and the price at the moment they matter most. The sheet keeps both behind it.
Polymarket in the same frame. Switch the venue above. Chart becomes a YES/NO outcome line, header swaps volume for resolution date, action bar becomes Back YES / Back NO, and the position row reads in shares. Frame, spacing and navigation do not move. This was the risk I flagged — it fits.

States

Specified on the detail screen; the same pattern applies everywhere.

  • Loading keeps the last price. The header shows what we knew; only the chart is a skeleton. A blank header makes a working app look broken.
  • Errors are inline, with retry. Never a popup — the user stays on the screen and the rest of it still works.
  • No wallet explains itself. Not a greyed-out button with no reason; the action bar becomes a single Connect action.
  • No stories means no rail. Not an empty card saying "no news" — reserved empty space is the most common way these screens get ugly.
The rule: a state never costs the user information they already had. Keep the last known value, replace only what is genuinely unknown.

Profile is the biggest cleanup: 7,133 lines across three standalone screens plus Polymarket's seven-file folder. They use the same words — positions, balance, PnL, deposit, withdraw, history — in three different layouts.

Profile · one portfolio

Venues supply rows and actions, not layout.

  • Account value leads, with all-time change under it. Same shape as the detail header so the app feels like one thing.
  • Deposit and Withdraw are always visible, not buried in a menu. They are the two actions people come here to find.
  • Three tabs, fixed: Positions, Orders, History. Every venue fills the same three; a venue without orders shows an empty state rather than a different tab set.
  • Rows are venue-worded. Phoenix shows size and leverage; Polymarket shows shares and chance. The row shape is identical.
  • Tapping a position opens the sheet — the same component as the detail screen, pre-filled to close rather than open.
Open question for sub-issue D. Phoenix has TP/SL, Polymarket has redeem-when-resolved, Meteora has range rebalancing. These do not generalize. Proposal: one "venue actions" slot on the position row that each adapter fills — rather than forcing a common vocabulary that fits none of them.
