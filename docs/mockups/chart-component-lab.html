Interactive component study

One chart. Small, exact layers.

Phoenix proves the rendering contract. Screens choose data and controls. The chart handles coordinates, gestures, selection, and layers.

Scope note: marker and trade-bubble experiments in this lab are deferred to phase two and are not part of the shipping chart V1.

  • Render onlyNo API, venue, or token knowledge inside the chart.
  • Native touchPan, pinch, long press, pinned inspection, and live-edge recovery.
  • ComposableCandles, line, volume, current price, and trade markers are independent layers.

Keyboard: arrows inspect candles, Escape clears, End jumps live. Mouse wheel zooms. Double-click resets.
