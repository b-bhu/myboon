How this resolves

Fed decision · one outcome can win.
Decision

The market resolves from the announced change to the Federal Reserve target range at its next scheduled meeting.

Outcome definitions

No change means 0 bps. A 25 bps cut means exactly −25 bps. A 50+ bps cut means −50 bps or lower. Rate hike means any increase.

Primary source

Federal Reserve press release
