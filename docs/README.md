## Messaging Rules

- Product name: **myboon**
- Primary line: **myboon tells you why the market moved and lets you act in the
  same app.**
- Explain both parts of the problem: fragmented understanding and complicated,
  disconnected action.
- Lead with the user journey, not terms such as "AI knowledge graph," "entity
  manager," or "context-to-action platform."
- Describe the feed as researched updates and developing stories, not as a
  generic news feed.
- Describe myboon as a working beta with **no external users, validated demand,
  meaningful usage metrics, or revenue yet**.
- The myboon X account is an active discovery and feedback channel; it is not
  traction by itself.
- Use **I** for founder work and **myboon** for product/system behavior. Do not
  imply a larger founding team.
