# Labelling sheet: latest-updates eval set

Bibhu, this is the one-time labelling for [#629](https://github.com/b-bhu/myboon/issues/629). About 15 minutes.

For each entity below, decide what you would want the reader to get **right now**:

- `write`: the new items add something a reader has not seen (a number, a date, a decision or an actor that the last update lacks), and it is worth a short update.
- `hold`: not worth the reader's time now (it repeats the last update, is vague, has no clear thing to watch, or is noise).

Write your answer in [`labels.csv`](labels.csv), one row per entity: `slug,label,note`. Use only `write` or `hold`; the note is optional. Bots do not label. Anything left blank blocks the eval.

How to read an entry: **Last update** is the stand-in for what we last told the reader (the entity's newest item from before the window). **New items** are what arrived in the 72 h freeze window, newest first. Dates are the day the event happened (`occurred_at`). The full data, with every item and source link, is in [`snapshot.json`](snapshot.json).
